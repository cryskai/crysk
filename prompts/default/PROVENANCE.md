- **Modifications (2026-10-02):** Changed the `model:` frontmatter of all five hunter prompts (`deep-review-lifecycle.md`, `deep-review-logic.md`, `deep-review-parity.md`, `deep-review-reliability.md`, `deep-review-resilience.md`) from `sonnet` to `opus` (an alias the Claude CLI resolves, so no version is pinned). The model evidence is the 8-replicate harness above. Those findings were not refuter-checked, and they are a single-diff sample. `bun run refuter-probe` is not affected (the refuter was already `opus`).
- **Modifications (2026-10-02, effort):** Frontmatter only, in all six bundled agent prompts; the prompt bodies are untouched. The five hunter prompts (`deep-review-lifecycle.md`, `deep-review-logic.md`, `deep-review-parity.md`, `deep-review-reliability.md`, `deep-review-resilience.md`) go from `model: opus` (the entry above, which is kept as history) back to `model: sonnet` and gain `effort: xhigh`. `review-refuter.md` keeps `model: opus` and gains `effort: high`. Root cause (#299): pr-hero never passed `--effort`, so every step ran at the Claude CLI's implicit default, which is `high` except on Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5, where it is `medium` (https://code.claude.com/docs/en/model-config). When the `sonnet` alias moved to claude-sonnet-5-5 the hunters silently dropped a level and stopped exploring, which the earlier `opus` change papered over rather than explained. The engine now owns the level (`src/model/catalog.ts`, `defaultEffortForRole`) and always passes it. Measured on musive pr-1858 (a 280-line PR), Sonnet 5.5, 4 hunters x 2 replicates, varying only the effort: `medium` 0/8 runs with findings (0.5 min per review, 0 of 4 known bugs); `high` 6/8 (1.5 min, 3 of 4); `xhigh` 8/8 (4.5 min, 4 of 4); `max` 7/8 (19 min, 4 of 4). `xhigh` is the cheapest level that found everything, so the hunters pin it. The refuter is `high`, not `medium`: `bun run refuter-probe` passed 16/16 at `medium`, but on the real pr-1858 `xhigh` findings the Opus refuter at `medium` returned `refuted` on the pre-labeled known `updateProject` BLOCKER in 3 of 4 verdicts, which deletes it, while at `high` it was `downgraded-latent` (kept, as advisory) in 3 of 4 and refuted in 1 of 4; the other 5 findings got the same verdicts at both levels, and the original Sonnet-5-at-high refuter corroborated that finding. The cost of `high` is a refuter leg of about 140s instead of about 90s. Guarded by `test/review/preflight-bundled-prompts.test.ts` (hunters declare sonnet + xhigh, the refuter declares opus + high, pinned against `localReviewSpec`'s hunter list). `bun run fixture-eval` result: pending (orchestrator).
